# Editorial source review — 15 October 2026

Scope: 45 selected topics, 270 Hindi/English recall questions, January–September 2026 (30 questions per month). The current-event claims were cross-checked against official institutional pages, including PIB, ICC, IPL, NASA, WHO, UN, UNEP, UNESCO, CBD, CONI and the airport operator. This is an editorial source check, not independent subject-expert certification or exhaustive coverage. No claim of predicted examination questions or exact exam alignment is made.

Some official sites rejected direct fetches. For World Water Day, International Women’s Day, World Oceans Day, World Press Freedom Day and the EEP tiger campaign, official-domain search-indexed text was also used. These are limitations of retrieval, not independent corroboration. Source pages may change. Counts and roles are tied to the source date.

Changes from supplied draft:
- Clarified scheduled/announced wording for Republic Day, Uttar Pradesh Day and the AI summit.
- Reworded the English T20 opponent question to refer unambiguously to India.
- Recast the tobacco-free school-campus answer as an application example: the retrieved campaign page did not establish it as a direct quotation or instruction to schools.
- Replaced the inaccessible UNESCO hub URL and UNEP URL with the official versions located in search.
- Added English revision notes and labels for selected background/application items.
- Preserved distinctions between airport inauguration and operations, approved spending and spending delivered, event-specific themes and global themes, and official language and national language.

No unsupported sports result was assumed from the draft: the ICC final report and official IPL final report were opened and checked, including winner, opponent, venue, result and captain/title count where asked. Their contents support the retained answers.

Background facts and simple conceptual examples are included alongside event recall. Arithmetic examples (10 years since 2016, 79 years since 1947, inclusive observance editions) are derived rather than separately announced events. The app does not claim every background answer is quoted verbatim by the linked event page.
